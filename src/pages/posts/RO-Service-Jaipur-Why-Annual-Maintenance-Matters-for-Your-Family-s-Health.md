## **Understanding Jaipur’s Unique Water Challenges**

Jaipur’s water supply comes from multiple sources, including groundwater and treated surface water. However, aging infrastructure and high mineral content create significant challenges. Around 70% of residents receive water with high TDS levels, and bacterial contamination has been found in about 30% of tested samples .

This mix of hardness, dissolved solids, and potential microbial contamination means standard filtration isn’t enough. RO systems effectively address all three issues, but the membranes and filters work harder here than in cities with softer water. Consequently, components wear out faster, making regular service a practical necessity rather than an optional expense.

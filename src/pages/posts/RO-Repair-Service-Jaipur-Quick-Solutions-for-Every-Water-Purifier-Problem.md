## **Common RO Problems in Jaipur Homes**

The unique water composition in Jaipur creates specific challenges for water purifiers. High TDS levels cause membranes to work overtime, leading to faster wear and tear. Sediment buildup is another frequent issue, particularly in areas where the municipal supply contains suspended particles. If you notice reduced water flow, unusual tastes, or frequent auto-shutoff activation, these are clear signals that your system needs professional evaluation.

Additionally, power fluctuations common in some parts of Jaipur can damage electronic control boards in modern purifiers. A technician with proper diagnostic tools can identify whether the issue is mechanical, electrical, or simply a matter of replacing consumable parts. Regular servicing prevents these minor issues from escalating into costly repairs.

## **RO Filter Change Jaipur – When and Why It Matters**

Filters are the heart of any water purification system, and they have a limited lifespan. The pre-filter typically lasts 6 to 12 months, while the carbon filter and post-filter need replacement every 12 months or sooner, depending on your water quality. If you are in need of [RO filter change Jaipur](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur), our experts recommend not waiting until the system displays an error code. By the time the warning appears, the filter may have already become a breeding ground for bacteria.

Changing filters at the right interval ensures your RO membrane receives clean water, which extends its life significantly. A fresh filter also improves water taste and reduces the workload on the pump. Many Jaipur residents notice immediate improvements in water clarity and taste after a professional filter replacement. ### **Signs Your Filters Need Changing**

- Water tastes metallic, salty, or unusual
- Slow water flow from the tap
- Cloudy or murky water after purification
- The system runs constantly or cycles on and off
- Unpleasant odor coming from dispensed water

## **How to Extend Your Purifier’s Life**

Beyond professional servicing, there are steps you can take to maximize your purifier's lifespan. Regular cleaning of the exterior prevents dust accumulation. Wiping the drip tray and checking for moisture underneath the system can catch leaks early. Additionally, avoid placing heavy objects on top of the purifier or exposing it to direct sunlight.

Using the system daily actually keeps the membrane active and prevents stagnation. However, if you leave town for extended periods, it is wise to flush the system upon return.

## **Understanding Your RO System's Components**

Modern RO water purifiers consist of several critical components working together. The pre-filter removes sediment and large particles, protecting the membrane from damage. The carbon filter eliminates chlorine and organic compounds that affect taste and odor. The RO membrane is the heart of the system, removing dissolved salts, heavy metals, and microorganisms. Finally, the post-filter polishes the water before it reaches your glass.

Each component has a specific lifespan and requires replacement at different intervals. Pre-filters typically need changing every 6 to 12 months, while carbon filters last about a year. The RO membrane, being the most expensive component, can last 2 to 3 years with proper maintenance. Understanding these timelines helps you plan for servicing and avoid unexpected breakdowns.

## **How to Extend Your Purifier's Life**

While professional servicing is essential, there are steps you can take to extend your purifier's life between technician visits. Keep the area around your purifier clean and dry to prevent dust accumulation and moisture damage. Avoid placing heavy items on top of the system. If you notice any unusual sounds or changes in water quality, contact ROCareIndia immediately rather than waiting for the scheduled service.

Also, be mindful of water pressure fluctuations. Very high pressure can damage the membrane and other components, while very low pressure affects purification efficiency. A pressure regulator can help maintain optimal conditions. Our technicians can advise you on appropriate measures for your home's specific plumbing setup.

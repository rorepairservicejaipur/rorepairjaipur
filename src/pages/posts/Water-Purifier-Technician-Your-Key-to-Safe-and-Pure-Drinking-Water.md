## **When to Call a Technician**

Certain signs indicate your purifier needs professional attention. If purified water taste changes noticeably, if flow rate drops significantly, or if the system makes unusual sounds during operation, a technician should inspect your unit promptly. Ignoring these warnings often leads to membrane damage or pump failure, resulting in costlier repairs.

Similarly, any visible leaks, electronic malfunctions, or persistent error codes require expert diagnosis. Attempting DIY repairs on these issues often voids warranties and risks damaging sensitive components.

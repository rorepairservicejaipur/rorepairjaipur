## **RO Membrane Replacement Jaipur – Restoring Peak Performance**

The RO membrane is the most critical component of your purification system. This semi-permeable membrane removes up to 99% of dissolved impurities, including heavy metals, nitrates, and bacteria. However, the membrane has a limited lifespan, typically 2 to 3 years depending on your water quality and usage patterns. When performance declines, professional [RO membrane replacement Jaipur](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur) becomes necessary to restore your system's effectiveness.

Signs that your membrane needs replacement include noticeably reduced water output, increased water wastage, poor water taste, or a sudden rise in TDS levels of purified water. ROCareIndia technicians test your water before and after membrane replacement to demonstrate the improvement, giving you complete confidence in the service.

### **Factors Affecting Membrane Lifespan**

- Incoming water TDS levels (higher TDS means faster wear)
- Frequency of filter changes (clogged filters damage the membrane)
- Water pressure variations
- Quality of pre-filtration
- Regular maintenance schedule

## **Maintaining Your Purifier Between Services**

While professional servicing is essential, you can take simple steps to keep your purifier healthy between visits. Wipe the exterior regularly to prevent dust accumulation. Check underneath the system for any moisture that might indicate a slow leak. If your system has a sediment filter, request a visual check during service visits.

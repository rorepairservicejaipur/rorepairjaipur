Jaipur's hard water puts your RO system through intense daily strain. The high mineral content quickly fills filters with sediment and causes membranes to work overtime. Many homeowners wonder whether they can maintain their purifiers themselves or whether professional help is necessary. Understanding basic maintenance goes a long way, but recognizing when to call experts is equally important. Your RO system consists of multiple filtration stages, each with specific maintenance needs. The pre-filter catches large particles, the carbon filter removes chlorine and odors, and the RO membrane handles dissolved salts and heavy metals. While you can perform some basic cleaning tasks at home, other components require professional attention to avoid damage and ensure continued water quality.

## **Understanding RO Membrane Lifespan in Jaipur**

The RO membrane is the heart of your purification system, responsible for removing up to 99% of dissolved impurities. However, [RO membrane lifespan](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur) varies significantly based on your water quality and usage patterns. In Jaipur, where TDS levels often exceed 1000 mg/L, membranes typically last between 18 to 24 months . This is considerably shorter than the 2 to 3 years often quoted in soft-water areas.

Several factors accelerate membrane degradation in Jaipur homes. High TDS forces the membrane to work harder, clogging pores faster. Sediment in the water supply can scratch the membrane surface. Irregular filter changes allow particles to reach the membrane, causing irreversible damage. And high water pressure can physically stress the membrane structure.

### **Signs Your Membrane Needs Replacement**

- Purified water TDS levels increasing noticeably
- Reduced water flow from the tap
- Increased water wastage during operation
- Unpleasant taste or odor in filtered water
- System running longer than usual to fill the storage tank

## **How Often to Service RO Purifier – A Practical Schedule**

Regular servicing is the single most important factor in extending your system's life. Understanding [how often to service RO purifier](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur) helps you plan maintenance and avoid unexpected breakdowns. For Jaipur residents with hard water, we recommend professional servicing every 4 to 6 months . This frequency allows technicians to catch issues early and replace consumables before they cause damage.

### **Recommended Service Intervals**

- **Pre-filter**: Replace every 6 months (sooner if water is very dirty)
- **Carbon filter**: Replace every 12 months or when taste changes
- **Post-filter**: Replace every 12 months or when taste changes
- **RO membrane**: Check every 6 months, replace every 18-24 months in Jaipur
- **UV lamp**: Replace every 12 months for optimal disinfection

## **How to Clean RO Filter at Home – Safe DIY Methods**

While professional servicing is essential, some light maintenance can be performed safely at home. Learning [how to clean RO filter at home](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur) helps maintain basic performance between professional visits. However, caution is essential as improper cleaning can damage sensitive components.

### **Safe Home Maintenance Tasks**

- Wipe the exterior with a soft, damp cloth to remove dust
- Clean the storage tank with mild detergent and water (follow manufacturer instructions)
- Check for visible leaks around connections
- Run a full flush cycle as per your system's manual

### **When to Stop and Call Professionals**

- Opening the system to access internal filters
- Cleaning or replacing the RO membrane
- Working on electrical components or pumps
- Dealing with persistent leaks after tightening connections
- Attempting to fix electronic control boards

Attempting these tasks without proper training voids warranties and risks permanent damage to your system. ROCareIndia technicians undergo extensive training and use specialized tools to safely service your purifier.

## **Common DIY Mistakes to Avoid**

Many homeowners inadvertently damage their systems while attempting DIY maintenance. Using harsh cleaning agents or bleach can destroy the RO membrane. Overtightening connections cracks housings. And mixing up filter positions can contaminate the entire system. Understanding these risks helps you make informed decisions about when to call professionals.
